Community wishlists and player experience feedback point toward several key platform upgrades needed to revolutionize throughput in the upcoming game:

1. **Curved Station Platforms:** Allowing modular track platforms to follow realistic natural terrain bends and gentle track curves without forcing rigid straight layouts.
2. **Multi-Level / Subterranean Interchanges:** Stacking metro platforms beneath heavy rail terminals and elevated light rail loops within a single unified facility.
3. **Dynamic Platform Allocation:** Allowing incoming trains to select any vacant platform on a designated approach line, preventing artificial traffic jams caused by rigid single-track assignments.
4. **Separated Boarding and Alighting:** Utilizing Spanish solutions (boarding from an island platform while alighting passengers exit onto an outer platform) to cut passenger dwell times in half.
