## Optimization and Fleet Management Tips

Running a fleet of rotorcraft requires disciplined maintenance scheduling. Helicopters suffer faster condition decay per kilometer than standard rolling stock due to mechanical complexity. Letting maintenance levels dip below good condition increases breakdown risks, which quickly backs up your single-pad terminals and leaves subsequent flights hovering in expensive holding patterns.

To ensure consistent cash flow, configure dedicated refueling and maintenance depots outside high-value urban terminal spaces. Position maintenance facilities on cheaper outskirts land along natural approach corridors so that aircraft can repair without disrupting active passenger pads.

Follow this sequential workflow when launching a new rotorcraft service to guarantee profitability from the very first month of operation:

| Phase Step | Operational Task | Key Performance Indicator (KPI) | Primary Risk Factor |

| **Step 1: Topography Survey** | Measure straight-line vs surface path length | Savings ratio > 2.5:1 | Terrain too flat for viable margin |

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do Transport Fever 3 helicopters compare to conventional feeder buses?
While buses feature minimal capital costs and high total capacity, they are vulnerable to street congestion and geographic delays. Rotorcraft carry fewer passengers per vehicle and demand higher operating expenses, but they fly entirely above road traffic and sheer elevation changes. Using helicopters as direct express feeders between remote enclaves and primary transit hubs generates higher ticket yields and drastically cuts journey duration.

### Can helicopters use standard airport runways?
In standard operational mechanics, rotorcraft utilize dedicated helipads and vertical landing markers rather than active airplane runways. While large international airports can feature auxiliary helipads along their perimeter aprons, helicopters cannot share physical takeoff queues with long-distance jetliners, ensuring that your regional air traffic does not disrupt high-volume long-haul flights.

### What is the most effective cargo to transport using rotorcraft?
Helicopters perform best when moving high-value, low-weight commodities like refined goods, electronics, tools, and mail over rugged terrain. Transporting low-margin bulk materials—such as raw stone or unprocessed coal—proves uneconomical via rotorcraft unless an isolated extraction site has zero viable rail or road access.

### How do I prevent airspace congestion around my central city skyports?
To avoid mid-air holding bottlenecks around busy downtown terminals, upgrade single landing pads to multi-pad configurations with dedicated approach and departure corridors. Additionally, enforce line spacing rules in your line management window so that departing flights clear the landing deck before incoming traffic enters the final approach glide path.
