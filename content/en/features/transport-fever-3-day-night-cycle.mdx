## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can players disable or freeze the transport fever 3 day night cycle?
Yes. Players who prefer constant daylight for building or benchmarking can toggle time progression in the game settings. You can lock the sun at a specific angle (such as constant midday) or freely adjust the speed of the diurnal cycle independently of the calendar year progression.

### Does the day-night transition change passenger demand automatically?
Based on player experience in early builds, the system models dynamic passenger flow. Stations experience distinct morning and evening surges reflecting realistic work commutes, followed by significant drops during late-night hours, creating clear windows for freight movements.

### Do vehicle headlights affect hardware performance significantly?
While headlights are dynamic light sources, modern rendering pipelines utilize deferred shading to minimize performance overhead. However, in massive multi-track yards with dozens of active engines, reducing headlight shadow quality in the graphics menu provides a noticeable frame rate improvement.

### How does the day-night cycle interact with the calendar year?
The time-of-day progression is decoupled from historical calendar dates. A single day-night cycle does not equal a full calendar year; instead, players can configure how many diurnal cycles occur before the in-game calendar advances, preserving realistic operational pacing across historical eras.
