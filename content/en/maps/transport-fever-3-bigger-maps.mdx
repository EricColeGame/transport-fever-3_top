## Designing Realistic Logistics Networks on Massive Terrains

Building an efficient freight and transit network across extensive terrain demands a departure from standard localized designs. In compact worlds, players often default to simple point-to-point lines because industries sit only a few kilometers apart.

On large-scale layouts, transportation networks require sophisticated hierarchical structures. Local commuter lines feed intermediate regional stations, which in turn feed high-capacity express hubs. Freight must transition from collection yards to heavy intermodal freight trunks before making the final urban delivery.

| Logistics Architecture | Best Application on Vast Maps | Primary Advantages | Common Challenges |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hub-and-Spoke Regional** | Intercity passenger links | Maximizes line capacity, reduces track clutter | Transfer congestion at main passenger hubs |
| **Dedicated Freight Trunks** | Primary raw material delivery | High throughput via 400m+ freight trains | Signal spacing balance and elevation management |
| **Point-to-Point High-Speed** | Distant metropolitan capitals | Maximum passenger profits per trip | Extreme construction costs over terrain |
| **Feeder Branch Lines** | Remote rural industries & towns | Cheap connectivity for low-demand goods | Vulnerable to unprofitable maintenance costs |

With **transport fever 3 bigger maps**, players can finally build mainline bypass tracks, dynamic holding sidings, and multi-tier marshalling yards without exhausting usable terrain. This added space permits natural curves, gentle grades, and realistic track layouts that mimic real-world civil engineering.

### Will transport fever 3 bigger maps require more system memory?
Yes. Larger terrain footprints, higher counts of simulated agents, and extensive vehicle lines naturally consume more RAM and VRAM. A minimum of 32 GB of system RAM is recommended to handle massive procedural generation and late-game traffic without memory thrashing.

### Can consoles handle transport fever 3 bigger maps?
While modern consoles feature powerful unified memory architectures, hardware limits may necessitate conservative map ceilings compared to high-end PCs. Developers often include custom map-size toggles or scalable agent-density settings to ensure smooth performance across all platforms.

### How do bigger maps affect vehicle profitability?
Vehicles earn revenue based on the distance traversed and the cargo or passengers transported. On larger maps, long-distance express passenger lines and full-length intermodal freight trains can yield massive payouts, provided signal management prevents delays and trains run near full capacity.
