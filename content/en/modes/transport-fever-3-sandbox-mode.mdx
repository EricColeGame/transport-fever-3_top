## Step-by-Step: Designing Realistic Rail Networks in Sandbox Mode

Creating an authentic rail network requires deliberate planning. Without financial penalties to constrain you, it is easy to build unrealistically steep inclines or chaotic spaghetti junctions. To build layouts that look and feel grounded in real-world engineering, follow a structured design process.

```
Phase 1: Regional Surveying  -->  Phase 2: Mainline Corridors  -->  Phase 3: Station Placement  -->  Phase 4: Junction Engineering  -->  Phase 5: Detailing & Props
```

Here is a practical breakdown of how to structure your network development lifecycle:

| Stage | Primary Objective | Key Tools Used | Pro Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Regional Surveying** | Identify natural corridors, valleys, and riverbeds | Contour view, terrain brush | Work with the geography; avoid tunneling through every obstacle |
| **2. Trunk Line Spine** | Establish high-capacity multi-track passenger lines | Parallel track tool, curve smoother | Keep ruling grades below 2.5% for realistic train acceleration |
| **3. Terminal Siting** | Position dead-end terminals or bypass through-stations | Modular station editor | Leave 4–6 empty platform slots for future expansion lines |
| **4. Yard & Branch Routing** | Build marshalling yards and secondary branch links | Slip switches, crossover tools | Add holding sidings near freight hubs to stage idle consists |
| **5. Scenic Detailing** | Integrate fencing, catenary gantries, and ground textures | Asset brush, landscaping tools | Layer ballast and gravel textures around switches to simulate wear |

Using **transport fever 3 sandbox mode** to test complex track arrangements lets you catch signaling bottlenecks before adding dozens of active trains to the route.

## Performance Optimization and Large-Map Best Practices

Sandbox mode encourages expansive building, but massive networks populated by thousands of independent agents can test your hardware. Community reports suggest that uncapped populations and overly dense pathfinding networks are the leading causes of late-game frame rate drops.

Use the strategies outlined below to maintain fluid performance across large and megalomaniac map sizes:

| Performance Factor | Risk Area | Recommended Sandbox Workaround |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Agent Pathfinding** | Massive interconnected road grids | Use disconnected satellite towns linked exclusively by rail |
| **Station Congestion** | Thousands of passengers waiting on one platform | Increase train lengths or frequency to keep platform counts low |
| **Line Signaling Checks** | Extremely tight signal spacing on high-speed lines | Space block signals 1.5 to 2 train lengths apart |
| **Asset Density** | Millions of manually painted trees and decorative props | Concentrate detailed foliage close to rail cuts; use low-poly backdrop terrain |
| **Depot Clutter** | Hundreds of idle consists stored in active yards | Delete unused test consists rather than parking them indefinitely |

Balancing aesthetic ambition with engine constraints ensures that your showcase world remains smooth and enjoyable to navigate during cab-ride camera sessions.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I earn achievements while playing in Transport Fever 3 sandbox mode?
By default, enabling sandbox mode and creative cheat tools disables standard platform achievements. However, player experience shows that certain custom scenario challenges and community workshop mods may re-enable specific milestone tracking depending on your exact configuration.

### How do I access all historical vehicles immediately in sandbox mode?
Within the sandbox world settings menu, toggle off the "Era Vehicle Lock" option. This unlocks every train, bus, truck, aircraft, and ship across all eras instantly, allowing you to run classic 19th-century steam locomotives alongside modern high-speed bullet trains.

### Can I convert an existing standard save game into a sandbox mode save?
Yes. You can load an existing career or free play save file and activate **transport fever 3 sandbox mode** via the custom game options before launching. Keep in mind that once a save is converted and saved with sandbox rules, standard economic rules cannot always be cleanly restored.

### What is the best way to prevent cities from sprawling over my rail tracks?
Use the town editor tools in sandbox mode to mark specific zones around your tracks as non-constructible easements, or place decorative fences, collision assets, and buffer terrain around your right-of-way to stop automated urban building expansion.
